## Description — English
Order fast delivery in Baku from your favourite restaurants, markets, pharmacies and flower shops with Go Route.

• Wide choice: restaurant meals, groceries, drinks, flowers, pharmacy items and more
• Live tracking: follow your courier on the map in real time
• Easy payment: cash on delivery or card via the courier's terminal
• Saved addresses, order history and notifications
• Favourites for quick reordering
